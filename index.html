Xuhua Huang

I am a Research Scientist at Luma AI, focusing on Physical AI. Currently cooking up some exciting work on agentic robot policies and world action models (WAMs).

Before Luma, I was a Senior Applied Research Scientist at Meta Reality Labs for four years, building large-scale multimodal generative models for Codec Avatars. I was a graduate student at Robotics Institute of Carnegie Mellon University, advised by Prof. Kris Kitani and Prof. Deva Ramanan. I received my Bachelor's degree from Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (First Class Honors), where I worked with Prof. Chi Keung Tang, Prof. Qifeng Chen and Prof. Yu-Wing Tai.

My interests include physical AI, robot learning, world models, multimodal generative models and 3D vision.
